---
title: Why I like small tools
date: 2026-04-18
description: A short note on picking boring, small dependencies over frameworks.
lang: en
---

Every project I start these days, I ask the same question: does this need a framework, or does it need a library?

Frameworks want to own your architecture. Libraries just do one thing and get out of the way. Lately I've been defaulting to the latter — a router here, a query cache there — instead of reaching for the batteries-included option.

It's slower to assemble at first. But six months in, I'm not fighting abstractions I didn't choose.
